Accessibility Statement

we sees customers with
physical, sensory, and cognitive disabilities as equal rights customers. There is no one who should not remember to have unrestricted access to the assets, information, and services we offer. Consequently, we place the issue of accessibility on our website at the top of our priority list.

In this statement, our goal is to streamline the use and improve our service regarding accessibility and equal rights for people with disabilities. Our accessibility adaptation was carried out in accordance with Regulation 35 of the Equal Rights for People with Disabilities (Accessibility Adjustments to Service) Regulations, 2013, to the AA level subject to changes and adjustments made in the Israeli Standard document. The accessibility adaptation was tested in browsers such as Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Mozilla, and Edge.

Accessibility features available on the site:

  • Support for all standard accepted browsers (such as Chrome, Explorer, FireFox, Opera, Mozilla).
  • The site’s content is written in clear language and uses readable fonts.
  • The site structure is built from headings, paragraphs, and lists.
  • Navigating the site is simple and comfortable, including accessible and clear menus.
  • Links on the site are clear and explain where they will lead after clicking on them.
  • Links at the top of the page that allow skipping to content.
  • Textual description for images and icons for assistive technologies.
  • Site adaptation to different work environments (responsiveness).
  • Stop and play buttons for video galleries.
  • Implementation of ARIA rules that help to interpret the site’s content more accurately and better.
  • Accessibility of menus, forms, fields, heading hierarchies, tab components, pop-up windows, and more.
  • Site display changes.
  • The site’s display can be enlarged or reduced by clicking on one of the “CTRL” buttons along with the mouse wheel or together with the “+” sign for enlargement or together with the “-” sign for reducing the display. Each click will decrease or increase the screen by ten percent (10%).
  • Font size change is made using the accessibility menu found on the site.

Users who do not have a mouse or cannot use a mouse can activate the features found on the site by pressing the “TAB” key. Each press will move the cursor to the next option on the site; pressing the “Enter” key will activate the link on which the cursor is located.

The site does not include flashes, scrolling, and moving content. In places where such content is found, it can be stopped by hovering over them and clicking the mouse or by navigating to them using the “TAB” key and pressing the “Enter” key.

Site adaptation for vision and hearing disabilities

  • Basic vision magnifiers (resolution)
  • Voice recognition software

Voice recognition packages of operating systems

  • Limitations to accessibility

Accessibility arrangements in the company

we does not physically receive the public. We aim to provide all customers, with or without disabilities, with equal and accessible service. However, despite our extensive efforts to make all pages on the site accessible, it is possible that specific parts may not be accessible. If such parts are found, you can contact our accessibility coordinator (whose details appear below).

We continue to work continuously on improving the accessibility of the site, out of a desire and commitment to allow the entire population, including people with disabilities, efficient and smooth use of our website.
