About Code Beneath

About

As a Software Engineer, my journey began in 1998 as a C/C++ and Visual Basic UI developer. Over the years, I transitioned to an application DBA role, where I managed large datasets of nearly 40 billion records. My background also includes low-level development for hardware access.

A significant milestone in my career was being the first person in Israel to develop a new methodology for working with SAP, which was successfully tested at Israel Aerospace Industries. Since 2006, I have focused on full-time Java development, contributing to various projects across multiple technologies.

This site has been created to guide individuals on how to use these technologies effectively and to help find solutions and technologies that align with their real requirements.
